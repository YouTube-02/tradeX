FAQ

Is this real trading?

No. Prices are generated by a simulator and balances are virtual.

Can I deposit or withdraw?

No. Payment features are disabled in this demonstration.

What is an OTC market?

Here, OTC assets use a separate synthetic price feed with its own candle patterns (trend legs, pullbacks, consolidations and pin-bar wicks) and stays open when Forex is closed on weekends. The feed is generated from the clock, so every user sees the same prices.

Who can register?

Only adults who meet the minimum age; eligibility must be checked on a server.
